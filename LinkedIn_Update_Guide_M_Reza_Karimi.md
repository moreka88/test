## 12. Featured section and one post

Add the 2022 and 2023 open-access papers (both are on Springer) to Featured, plus the 2025 paper. Then publish one post; your profile shows zero post impressions, and activity helps search ranking. This draft uses only published results:

```
Physical loss or dissolution? A question I spent years on.

When we deposited wear-resistant Ni-WC overlays for mining equipment, a large share of the tungsten carbide in the feed wire never ended up in the coating. The usual explanation is that carbide dissolves in the hot weld pool. High-speed imaging showed a second cause: particles were blown out of the process zone, at arc re-ignition and when the wire retracted. We estimated this physical loss at up to ~44% of the carbide in the wire under some conditions.

Two takeaways:
1. If you only measure what is left in the coating, you cannot tell why material is missing.
2. The right fix depends on which mechanism dominates.

Our comparison of cold metal transfer and conventional short-circuit GMAW is open access in the International Journal of Advanced Manufacturing Technology: https://doi.org/10.1007/s00170-023-11955-4
```
